# 7 · Choose a low-carbon region

**In one line:** when you can choose where your requests run, choose a region
with low-carbon electricity.

This lever applies only if you can choose. Most coding assistants do not let you
choose the region. The lever is for cloud providers and your own models.

## Why it matters

The same energy emits very different amounts of CO₂, depending on the country
of the data centre. Carbon intensity of electricity in 2025
([Our World in Data / Ember](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/carbon-intensity-electricity)):

| Country or region | gCO₂ per kWh |
|---|---:|
| Norway | 28 |
| Sweden | 35 |
| France | 41 |
| European Union | 210 |
| United States | 384 |
| World | 458 |
| China | 525 |
| Poland | 589 |
| India | 670 |

From Norway to India, the gap is more than 20 times.

## How to apply

You can choose the region when:
- you use a model through a cloud provider that lets you choose a region (for
  example Amazon Bedrock or Google Cloud), which Claude Code can use;
- you run your own models or AI services.

Then choose a region with low-carbon electricity, if your rules on data location
allow it.

**Teams:** add carbon intensity to the criteria when you choose a provider and a
region. Prefer providers that publish data on their energy use and emissions.

## Find the data

- [Google Cloud region carbon data](https://cloud.google.com/sustainability/region-carbon):
  carbon data for each Google Cloud region.
- [Electricity Maps](https://www.electricitymaps.com/): carbon intensity of
  electricity, live and for each zone.

## Resources

- [Carbon intensity of electricity](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/carbon-intensity-electricity)
  (Our World in Data, from Ember): values for each country and year.
